## Reading

- **Offroad is VaVAM-B's dominant failure, and it happens immediately:** 40–50%
  of rollouts, a median of 1.8–2.5 s after the handover from log replay, at only
  ~2 m from the human path. This is the early drift seen open loop (rightward
  bias, `reports/m1_b0a_b0b_valcl.md`), not a late loss of control.
- **Front collisions are second** (9–22%), mostly rear-ending slower traffic.
  Lateral collisions are rare, and no rear collisions are counted (they are not
  at-fault).
- **The AIS adapter shifts failures toward front collisions** (9 → 16 of 96 on
  eval_big-48) and adds 4 off-map aborts. That fits its faster driving, from the
  executed-speed input (`reports/m3_closed_loop.md`): it keeps speed and closes
  on lead vehicles.
- A policy improvement has to act in the first ~2 s after handover and
  laterally. Neither imitation of human replays nor the latent RL reached that.
